Put your execution page on your home screen

There's nothing to download. It's this page, pinned so it opens like an app. Everyone uses the same link and each person sees only their own email and calendar.

iPhone

  1. Open the link in Safari and sign in to claude.ai.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Keep the name Owner Execution Pages and tap Add.

Android

  1. Open the link in Chrome and sign in to claude.ai.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  4. Keep the name and tap Add.

Computer

  1. Open the link in Chrome or Edge and sign in to claude.ai.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the three-dot menu and choose Cast, save and share, then Install page as app (Edge: Apps, then Install this site as an app).
  3. It opens in its own window. Right-click its icon to pin it to the taskbar or dock.

Good to know

  1. Stay signed in to claude.ai in that browser. Signing out disconnects the page.
  2. The page refreshes itself each time you open it and every 10 minutes while it's open.
  3. Sorting your inbox and writing drafts uses your own Claude plan.
  4. If you see someone else's email, stop and tell Sam. It should never happen.
