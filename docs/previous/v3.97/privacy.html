WYZ Execution Page: privacy

The WYZ Execution Page is a private tool for WYZ Rent, its team and people they invite. It shows your own email, calendar, files and tasks in one place.

What it reads

Only what you allow when you sign in with Google or Microsoft: your email, calendar, files you choose to open, and contacts for address suggestions. You can switch each source off at any time in Settings.

Where your data goes

Your email, calendar and files go straight from Google or Microsoft to your own browser. They aren't stored on our servers. Settings and your task list are kept in your browser on your device.

If you use the AI features, the text you ask about is sent to the AI service you chose (Anthropic Claude or OpenAI ChatGPT) to write the answer. It isn't used for anything else.

Sending and changes

Nothing is sent or changed in your accounts unless you press a button to do it, and sending always asks you to confirm.

Removing access

Tap Sign out in the page, or remove the app from your Google or Microsoft account security settings. You can also clear your browser's site data to delete everything kept on your device.
